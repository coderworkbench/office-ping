# Release history

What changed in each version of Office Ping, newest first. The same list is in the app: **Help → What's new**.

## 1.6.0 — 2026-11-06

**Emoji and reactions in chat**

- **New:** An emoji button next to the message box, with about 150 emojis in five tabs.
- **New:** React to a message with 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 or **＋** for more: point at it on a computer, or press and hold on a phone. One reaction each; pick again to change or remove it.
- **New:** A quiet desktop notification when someone reacts to your message, unless that chat is open, muted, or you're *In a meeting* or on *Do not disturb*.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-06

**A sound for chat messages**

- **New:** A new chat message plays a short, soft *blip-blip*, quieter than a ping and easy to tell apart from it.
- **New:** No sound for the chat you already have open, for chats you've muted, or while you're *In a meeting* or on *Do not disturb*. Several messages in a row make one sound.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-06

**A louder ping sound and security updates**

- **Improved:** Pings are much louder and easier to recognize: a rising three-note bell for normal pings and a sharp alarm for urgent ones.
- **Fixed:** Pings that arrived before you'd clicked in the Office Ping window made no sound. Now one click anywhere in the window turns sound on for the day.
- **Security:** Updated the software Office Ping is built on to fix known security issues.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-25

**Messages: one-to-one chat**

- **New:** **Messages** in the sidebar and phone tab bar: a one-to-one chat for things that don't need an answer this minute.
- **New:** Start a chat from **People** (**Message**) or with **New message**. See *typing…* while they write and **Seen** once they've read it.
- **New:** On a computer, a **Messages** bar in the bottom-right corner keeps your chats one click away on every page.
- **New:** **Ping** at the top of a chat sends that person a ping when it can't wait. The bell mutes a chat.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-17

**Your status, quick replies and urgent reminders**

- **New:** Set a status (*In a meeting*, *Do not disturb*, *Out for lunch*, *Away from desk*) with an end time. In a meeting or on Do not disturb, normal pings wait quietly in your Inbox.
- **New:** Quick replies when you acknowledge a ping, shown to the sender straight away and in History.
- **New:** An urgent ping nobody has acknowledged after 5 minutes pops up again, and the sender is told.
- **New:** Press **Ctrl + K** or **/** to ping someone from any page. History has search and filters; admins can export it as CSV.
- **New:** Office Ping can open by itself when your computer starts (see *Help*).
- **New:** HR and admins choose which three templates appear on everyone's Overview.
- **Improved:** A phone Overview that fits on one screen, with people, quick messages and Send together.
- **Improved:** Repeated pings collapse into one (*Nudge ×8*) and can be acknowledged together.
- **Security:** Limits on how often pings can be sent, and sign-in is locked for a while after repeated wrong passwords.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-16

**Pop-up pings, Help and a new look**

- **New:** A ping now pops up in the middle of the screen and stays until you click **Acknowledge** or **Later**. Urgent pings jump the queue.
- **New:** The **Help** page: a user manual with animated demos, and a guided tour (**Show me around**).
- **Improved:** The Sunbeam look, and every page now works on phones and tablets.
- **Fixed:** Pings no longer show up more than once, and acknowledging on one device clears the pop-up on the others.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-15

**First release**

- **New:** Send a ping to a person or a group, normal or urgent, and see when it's delivered and acknowledged.
- **New:** Inbox, History, groups and message templates, with desktop notifications.
- **New:** Change your own password; admins can reset someone else's.
